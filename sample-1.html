Plate 06SAGE OS

A disciplined framework from diagnosis to action.

  1. S

    Scan

    Observe the system

    Examine the people, purpose, setting, experience and conditions shaping everyday life.

  2. A

    Analyse

    Understand what is influencing the system

    Identify patterns, relationships and gaps across biology, environment and behaviour, and understand what may be driving the problem.

  3. G

    Generate

    Design integrated possibilities

    Develop practical ideas and strategies, grounded in evidence, that respond to the system as a whole.

  4. E

    Execute

    Translate insight into practice

    Implement, test and refine decisions in real-world settings.

The objective is not more activity. It is a clearer understanding of the system, better decisions and interventions that can hold under real operating conditions.

  • A project brief and design recommendations
  • A programme or environment strategy
  • An implementation roadmap
  • A current-state assessment and gap analysis
  • A prioritised action plan
  • Decision briefs and evidence reviews
  • Workshops, talks and leadership briefings
  • A board briefing or strategic review

Scope and deliverables are agreed at the outset of each engagement.
